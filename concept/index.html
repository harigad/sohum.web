The name

So on the inhale.
Hum on the exhale.

In Sanskrit, so’ham means I am that. It is the sound the breath makes when no one is listening, the oldest and simplest of mantras.

We built an object around it.

The Instrument

Three things,
made to disappear.

I

The Bowl

Frosted quartz crystal that rings with a single, unwavering note. Its tone doesn’t fill the room so much as clear it.

Material
Quartz crystal
Finish
Frosted
II

The Cradle

Formed layer by layer from fine wood fibre, so its form could follow the arc of a held breath rather than the limits of a lathe. Warm to the hand, and quietly exact.

Material
Printed wood
Form
Built layer by layer
III

The Resonance

Hidden within the cradle, a quiet system carries the bowl’s tone into the body as vibration, and lets a low light pool beneath it.

Nothing to see. Everything to feel.

The Ritual

A day,
held in three tones.

  1. Dawn

    Begin

    Before the phone, before the news. One strike, ten breaths, and the day starts on your terms.

  2. Dusk

    Return

    A threshold between work and home. Let the tone carry the day out of your shoulders.

  3. Night

    Rest

    Lights low, the cradle glowing. A slow descent into the quiet that comes before sleep.
